# Sources — every story on the page

Checked 23 November 2026 against reporting published on 23 November 2026.
Where an event itself happened on Sunday 22 November, the card says so in
its own words ("ends Sunday", "wins ... on Sunday", "reported Monday") —
nothing on the page is presented as first reported on 23 November unless
it was. Two stories that dominated 23 November coverage were deliberately
left out because they broke earlier and were not 23 November news: the
Riemann Hypothesis proof claim (announced 20 Nov, certified 21 Nov) and
the capture of "Chapo Isidro" (Friday night 20–21 Nov).

1. **COP31 climate talks collapse (Antalya, Turkey).** The summit ended
   Sunday 22 Nov after two days of overtime with no deal; the EU and about
   130 parties rejected a text that omitted fossil fuels. Reported Monday
   23 Nov. Sources: BBC ("COP31 ends without a major climate deal",
   23 Nov 04:01 UTC), The Hindu (23 Nov 1:05 am), One Turn 23 Nov edition.
2. **Nikkei dives 5.06% (Tokyo, Japan).** Asian stocks fell Monday 23 Nov
   as a US AI sell-off spread; Japan's Nikkei dropped 5.06% and Hong Kong
   hit a 19-month low. Sources: One Turn 23 Nov edition; SimpleLife.news
   23 Nov daily news.
3. **Democrats take the House (USA).** Democrats secured at least 258
   seats — their largest House majority since 1992 — after Chuck Hubbard
   beat Virginia Foxx in North Carolina's 5th district following a
   recount; the race was called Sunday 22 Nov (AP, ~1:00 pm). Reported
   Monday 23 Nov. Sources: Reuters (22 Nov), Fox News, Washington
   Examiner, The Times.
4. **Russian warship sinks at Benghazi (Libya).** The Russian corvette
   Merkury sank at Benghazi naval base after a suspected Ukrainian sea
   drone attack. Reported Monday 23 Nov. Sources: The Hindu (23 Nov
   6:32 am), One Turn 23 Nov edition.
5. **Korea passes $1 trillion in exports (Seoul).** The $1 trillion
   threshold was crossed at 2:09 pm on 19 Nov; Korea Customs Service data
   released Monday 23 Nov showed $77.4bn exported 1–20 Nov (+101.5%) and
   a year total of $1.0076tn — the fourth country ever past $1tn, after
   the US, Germany and China. Sources: AJP (23 Nov), Korea Customs
   Service via Seoul Economic Daily (23 Nov), KuCoin News (23 Nov).
6. **Houthis claim Saudi strike (Rabigh / Riyadh).** Yemen's Houthis said
   Sunday 22 Nov they had hit the Rabigh oil refinery on the Red Sea
   coast (claiming a "direct hit") and a radar facility south of Riyadh;
   the claims could not be independently verified and Saudi authorities
   did not immediately comment. Reported Monday 23 Nov. Sources: Reuters
   (22 Nov), IANS via The Freedom Press (Sanaa, Nov 23), TBS News
   (23 Nov 08:40 am).
